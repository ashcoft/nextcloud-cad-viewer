# Installation from App Store

The easiest way to install Nextcloud CAD Viewer is through the Nextcloud App Store.

## Prerequisites

- Nextcloud 33 or higher
- Admin access to your Nextcloud instance

## Steps

1. **Navigate to Apps**
   - Log in to your Nextcloud instance as an administrator
   - Click on your profile icon in the top-right corner
   - Select "Apps" from the dropdown menu

2. **Find CAD Viewer**
   - Use the search bar to search for "CAD Viewer"
   - Alternatively, browse the "Tools" or "Multimedia" categories

3. **Install**
   - Click the "Download and enable" button next to CAD Viewer
   - Wait for the installation to complete

4. **Verify Installation**
   - The app should now appear in your list of enabled apps
   - Upload a DWG or DXF file to test the viewer

## Post-Installation

No additional configuration is required. The app works out of the box!

## Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues during installation:

- Ensure your Nextcloud instance meets the minimum requirements
- Check that you have sufficient disk space
- Verify your internet connection for downloading the app
- Review the Nextcloud logs for any error messages
